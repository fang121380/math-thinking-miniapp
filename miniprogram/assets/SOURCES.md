## Colored Thinking Rabbit

- File: `thinking-rabbit-color.png`
- Derivation: Deterministic local colorization of the CC0 Thinking Rabbit source; original pose, outline, dimensions, and transparent background preserved.
- Palette: Warm ivory, soft coral, and pale green from the product interface.
- Reproduction: `docs/tools/colorize-rabbit.py`
- Use: Colored guide character in child-facing pages.

## Published App Icon

- File: `app-icon-1024.png`
- Derivation: Square application icon composed from the CC0 Thinking Rabbit source above, with the product's light-green and coral palette.
- Output size: 1024 x 1024 PNG.
- Use: WeChat Mini Program publishing icon.
